Where and when, kept in every pic

Bring your photos and videos home with their dates.

Hic Pic Nunc is a free, open-source desktop app that exports the photos and videos you choose from Google Photos and keeps their capture dates, so they stay in the right order in any other photo library.

Download for macOS or Windows How to use it

Not a commercial product: no account, no subscription, no ads.

What it does

  • Sign in with Google, then pick photos and videos in Google’s own picker. The app only sees the items you pick.
  • Each photo keeps its EXIF capture date, and optionally its camera details. Items without one, and videos, get the date Google Photos records for them.
  • Everything is packaged in one ZIP: once extracted, every file’s date matches when it was taken.
  • It can also restore file dates on photos you already downloaded, using their EXIF data.

Why “Hic Pic Nunc”?

From the Latin hic et nunc, “here and now”: every photo is a moment in a place and a time. The app keeps that moment with the picture, the pic between hic and nunc.

Runs on your computer

The app runs locally and talks only to Google. There is no Hic Pic Nunc server: your photos, your Google sign-in and your settings never reach the developer or anyone else. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Limits set by Google

Google removes location (GPS) from every photo downloaded through its API, and provides a high-quality transcoded copy of each video, not the original file. For a full migration that keeps locations and original videos, Google Takeout is currently the only option.
